### Fact Pattern

AfyaConnect Ltd is a Kampala-based health technology company. Its platform connects patients to diagnostic laboratories, pharmacies, and general practitioners across Uganda. Patients register on the platform, book lab tests, and receive results electronically. The platform processes approximately 500 patient records per day.

AfyaConnect hosts its platform on infrastructure provided by **CloudServe UK Ltd**, a company registered in England and Wales. The terms of the arrangement are set out in **Attachment Document 1 (Cloud Services Agreement)**. AfyaConnect's application data is stored on servers physically located at CloudServe's data centre in Nairobi, Kenya.

On 10 July 2026, a patient complained that they could see another patient's lab results when logged into their account. AfyaConnect's internal review produced:

- **Attachment Document 2 (API Audit Log)** — showing API requests made on 10 July 2026
- **Attachment Document 3 (SDLC Deployment Log)** — showing how the Clinical Results module was built and deployed

AfyaConnect's managing director has called you for advice. No litigation has been filed yet.
