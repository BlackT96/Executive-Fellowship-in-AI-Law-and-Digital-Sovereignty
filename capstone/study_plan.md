# Study Plan — Executive Fellowship in AI Law & Digital Sovereignty

## How to Use This Plan

Each week, study the curriculum topic and simultaneously **practice with the corresponding agent(s)**. The agents are not passive references — they are the tool through which you apply each week's concepts.

**Two-stream approach:**
1. **Learn** — study the curriculum content for the week
2. **Apply** — invoke the corresponding agent(s) with real or hypothetical facts

**Three-stream with assessment:**
1. **Learn** — study the curriculum content for the week
2. **Apply** — invoke the corresponding agent(s) with real or hypothetical facts
3. **Assess** — use the Assessment Agent to generate and complete weekly formative quizzes (25 min), monthly end-of-module examinations (3 hrs, 100 marks, LDC-Style), and the final comprehensive examination (6 hrs, 200 marks)

The capstone scenarios show how agents combine for complex multi-week deliverables. The Assessment & Examination Agent (skills/teaching/assessment_agent.md) provides:
- **Weekly formative quizzes** (25 min, 15-20 marks) — drawn directly from the week's Relevance Assessment, Missing Links, and Foundation-to-Tune analysis
- **Monthly examinations** (3 hrs, 100 marks) — LDC-Style with problem questions, complex scenarios requiring IRAC analysis, and professional drafting tasks
- **Final examination** (6 hrs, 200 marks) — cross-module synthesis with legal opinion, legislative drafting, and written submissions
- **Answer marking** — upload answer sheets for automatic marking, corrections, recommendations, and cohort analytics
- **Cohort analytics** — anonymised peer comparison, per-question performance heatmap, and curriculum gap analysis

## Study Rhythm

```
Each week:
  Day 1-2: Read curriculum content for the week
  Day 3-4: Study the corresponding agent(s) — read their full spec
  Day 5-6: Apply — feed real/hypothetical facts into the agent(s), review outputs
  Day 7:  Consolidate — run the quality checklist from the agent, refine

Every 4 weeks (end of module):
  Compile deliverable from the module's weekly outputs
  Cross-reference with the capstone scenarios for multi-agent workflows
  Sit the monthly examination — use Assessment Agent Template B to generate the 3-hour, 100-mark LDC-Style module exam
  Upload your answer sheet to the Assessment Agent for marking using Template C
  Review corrections, recommendations, and curriculum gap analysis
  Compare performance with cohort via anonymised peer comparison report
  Complete remediation exercises for weak areas before starting the next module

End of programme (after Module 11):
  Sit the Final Comprehensive Examination — use Assessment Agent Template D to generate the 6-hour, 200-mark final exam
  Upload answer sheet for marking and Professional Readiness Assessment
  Receive final classification (Distinction/Merit/Pass/Fail) and certification recommendation
```
