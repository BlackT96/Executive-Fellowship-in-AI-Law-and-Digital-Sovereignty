# Multi-Agent Workflow Scenarios

These scenarios demonstrate how multiple agents combine to handle real-world Ugandan/East African legal matters end-to-end.

---

## Scenario 1: Data Breach Response

**Situation:** A Kampala-based fintech suffers a data breach exposing 50,000 customer records. The NDPC has issued a preliminary notice. The client needs breach notification, regulatory representation, and remediation strategy.

### Workflow

```
Trigger Event (Breach)
        │
        ▼
[1] Cybersecurity Compliance Agent
    ├── Classify breach (type, scope, severity)
    ├── Identify notification trigger (DPA s.14, s.22)
    └── Timeline analysis (72-hour notification window)
        │
        ▼
[2] Privacy Compliance Agent
    ├── Data subject notification template
    ├── NDPC breach report drafting
    └── Data subject rights assessment (art. 12-22 DPA)
        │
        ▼
[3] Litigation Strategy Agent
    ├── Class action risk assessment
    ├── Regulatory defence strategy
    └── Insurance coverage analysis
        │
        ▼
[4] Comparative Law Agent
    ├── Kenya Data Protection Act breach notification (parallels)
    ├── EU GDPR Art. 33/34 comparator
    └── Nigeria NDPR breach response (guidance)
        │
        ▼
[5] Legal Opinion Agent
    ├── Formal legal opinion on liability exposure
    └── Remediation recommendations
        │
        ▼
[6] Writing Agent (LinkedIn or Weekly Article)
    └── Optional: thought leadership piece on data breach readiness
```

**Outputs:** Breach notification letters, NDPC response, legal opinion, insurance notification, remediation roadmap.

## Scenario 2: AI Governance Framework for Government

**Situation:** A Ministry of ICT is developing a National AI Governance Framework. They need a comprehensive policy architecture covering ethical AI, data protection, sectoral regulation, and international alignment.

### Workflow

```
Strategic Initiative
        │
        ▼
[1] Technology Strategy Agent
    ├── National AI readiness assessment
    ├── Stakeholder mapping (MDAs, private sector, academia)
    ├── Global AI governance landscape scan
    └── Strategic recommendations
        │
        ▼
[2] Comparative Law Agent
    ├── EU AI Act analysis (risk-based tiering)
    ├── Rwanda AI governance framework
    ├── Kenya AI policy approach
    ├── Singapore AI governance model
    └── India AI regulatory framework
        │
        ▼
[3] AI Governance Agent
    ├── Ethical AI principles for Uganda
    ├── ISO 42001 alignment mapping
    ├── NIST AI RMF adoption framework
    ├── Sectoral deployment guidelines
    └── Enforcement and oversight mechanism design
        │
        ▼
[4] Regulatory Impact Agent
    ├── Regulatory impact assessment
    ├── Human rights impact assessment
    ├── Economic impact analysis
    └── Cost-benefit analysis of regulatory options
        │
        ▼
[5] Legislative Drafting Agent
    ├── AI Bill (primary legislation)
    ├── AI Regulations (subsidiary legislation)
    └── Explanatory memorandum
        │
        ▼
[6] Digital Sovereignty Agent
    ├── Data localisation requirements in framework
    ├── Sovereign AI compute infrastructure recommendations
    └── Cross-border data flow provisions
        │
        ▼
[7] Writing Agent (Weekly Article)
    └── Publication: "Uganda's Path to AI Governance"
```

**Outputs:** National AI policy document, draft AI Bill, regulatory impact assessment, stakeholder consultation paper, implementation roadmap.

## Scenario 3: Technology Dispute — AI System Failure

**Situation:** A hospital deployed an AI diagnostic system that misdiagnosed patients. The hospital and the AI vendor are in a dispute. Patients are considering legal action. The system was provided by a foreign vendor under a Ugandan law-governed contract.

### Workflow

```
Dispute
  │
  ├──► [1] Contract Drafting Agent
  │       ├── Review SaaS/licence agreement
  │       ├── Identify warranty, limitation, indemnity clauses
  │       └── SLA compliance assessment
  │
  ├──► [2] Digital Evidence Agent
  │       ├── System logs preservation strategy
  │       ├── Algorithm audit trail analysis
  │       ├── Chain of custody protocol
  │       └── Forensic expert identification
  │
  ├──► [3] Litigation Strategy Agent
  │       ├── Cause of action analysis (breach of contract, negligence, product liability)
  │       ├── Defendant identification (vendor, hospital, developers)
  │       ├── Jurisdiction and choice of law assessment
  │       └── Strength of claim assessment
  │
  ├──► [4] Pleading Drafting Agent
  │       ├── Plaint drafting (if claimant)
  │       ├── Defence drafting (if defendant)
  │       ├── Expert witness statements
  │       └── Interim applications (preservation orders)
  │
  ├──► [5] Expert Witness Agent
  │       ├── Technical explanation of AI system failure
  │       ├── Expert report for court
  │       └── Cross-examination preparation
  │
  └──► [6] Legal Opinion Agent
          └── Formal opinion on liability and damages
```

**Outputs:** Litigation strategy memo, pleadings, expert reports, evidence preservation plan, legal opinion.

## Scenario 4: Cross-Border Data Transfer

**Situation:** A Ugandan bank wants to use a cloud provider based in the US and a call centre in Kenya. They need compliant data transfer mechanisms.

### Workflow

```
Client Instruction
        │
        ▼
[1] DPA Agent
    ├── Data mapping exercise
    ├── Processor vs. controller analysis
    ├── Standard Contractual Clauses (Uganda DPA-compliant)
    ├── Kenya data transfer assessment
    └── US cloud provider adequacy analysis
        │
        ▼
[2] Comparative Law Agent
    ├── Kenya Data Protection Act adequacy decision status
    ├── EU GDPR adequacy decisions (transfer tool equivalents)
    └── EAC data transfer harmonisation
        │
        ▼
[3] Digital Sovereignty Agent
    ├── Data localisation obligations (if any)
    ├── Sovereign cloud assessment
    └── Sensitive data classification
        │
        ▼
[4] Legal Opinion Agent
    ├── Formal opinion on transfer compliance
    └── Residual risk assessment
        │
        ▼
[5] Writing Agent (LinkedIn)
    └── Summary: "Data Transfers in East Africa — What Banks Need to Know"
```

**Outputs:** DPA with SCCs, data transfer impact assessment, legal opinion, regulatory filing (if required).

## Scenario 5: AI Law Curriculum Design

**Situation:** A Ugandan university wants to introduce a 12-week AI Law course as part of its LL.M programme.

### Workflow

```
Academic Initiative
        │
        ▼
[1] Curriculum Agent
    ├── Learning outcomes design
    ├── Module structure (12 weeks)
    ├── Assessment framework
    ├── Reading list
    └── Teaching methodology
        │
        ▼
[2] All Domain Agents (module inputs)
    ├── Week 1-2: Digital Technology Fundamentals (references)
    ├── Week 3-4: Privacy & Compliance (Privacy, Cybersecurity agents)
    ├── Week 5-6: AI Governance (DPIA, AI Governance, AI Audit agents)
    ├── Week 7-8: Litigation & Digital Evidence (Litigation agents)
    ├── Week 9: Digital Sovereignty (Digital Sovereignty agent)
    ├── Week 10: Policy & Legislation (Policy agents)
    ├── Week 11: Transactional Practice (Transactional agents)
    └── Week 12: Capstone & Writing (Writing agents)
        │
        ▼
[3] Book Writer Agent
    └── Curriculum-to-textbook conversion
```

**Outputs:** Course syllabus, module guides, assessment rubrics, reading lists, textbook draft.

        ▼

## Creating New Scenarios

To design your own multi-agent workflow:

1. **Identify the trigger event** (breach, instruction, dispute, policy initiative, academic project)
2. **Determine the primary domain** using the orchestrator classification key
3. **Identify support needs** (research, comparison, technical explanation, quality review)
4. **Chain agents** in logical order: Research → Draft → Review → Publish/File
5. **Define outputs** for each stage
