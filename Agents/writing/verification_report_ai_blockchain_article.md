## Key corrections carried into the draft

1. **FIA ranking:** "34th of 155 countries" must be attributed to the FIA National Risk Assessment report, not Chainalysis directly (Chainalysis' 2024 methodology ranked 151 countries).
2. **Remittance fees:** use ~8.5% (latest World Bank quarter), not 8.78%.
3. **Karnak:** "Egypt's first national LLM" — sources do not support "Africa's first national sovereign LLM."
4. **Judiciary AI:** the Judgment-Writing Tool is already launched by CJ Zeija (drafting-assist), not "being developed."
5. **UGGov Agent:** private-sector platform built by Afrosoft IT Solutions, not an official state system.
6. **Kenya court orders:** two — COFEK conservatory orders 11 Dec 2025 (Justice Bahati Mwamuye); Omtatah full suspension 19 Dec 2025 (Justice Chacha Mwita).
7. **Third National Data Centre:** removed entirely from the World Bank UDAP-GovNet project (-100%); only a 2025 NITA-U market study exists.
8. **Aeonian:** 10MW USIO supercomputer on NVIDIA Blackwell; 100MW is the campus design target.
9. **Paperless courts:** two dates reported — 1 June 2026 (Judiciary circular of 17 Feb 2026) vs 1 July 2026 (Observer). Cite per Observer or note both.
10. **US-Uganda MoU composition:** up to $1.7bn US funding + Uganda's ~$500m+ domestic increase = $2.3bn framework.
11. **Specimen sharing:** "within five days of a US request" (ProPublica) / "within five days of detection" (Monitor draft); term up to 25 years.
12. **PPDA (Cap. 205) AI gap** and **no dedicated AI budget line** are defensible negative *inferences* from public documents — state as inferences, not sourced facts.
